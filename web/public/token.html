$REAL: the coin of Realsies

Facts as of 11 November 2026. Everything on this page can be checked on chain; the links go to the explorer.

Token contract: real.realsies.near on NEAR mainnet.

REAL is the token behind the coins you play with on realsies.lol, the human-or-bot chat game. One coin in the game is 10,000 REAL.

The numbers

Name and symbolRealsies, REAL (18 decimals, a standard NEAR token)
Contractreal.realsies.near (explorer)
Supply1,000,000,000 REAL, fixed. There is no minting, and the token account has no keys, so nobody can change it.
In the pool800,000,000 REAL (80%) went into one pool on Rhea Finance at the launch. The account that owns that position, liquidity.realsies.near (explorer), has no keys, so nobody can take the liquidity out.

Launched10 November 2026, 15:04 UTC

Where it trades

  • In the game. "Get coins" on realsies.lol: you choose an amount of NEAR and approve once in your wallet. "Withdraw" turns coins back into NEAR the same way.
  • On Rhea Finance. The pool is real.realsies.near|wrap.near|2000 on dclv2.ref-labs.near: open the pool on Rhea.
  • Charts. GeckoTerminal and DexScreener.

What it is for

You chat with a character for two minutes, then call whether it was a real human or a bot. You can play free for rating. With coins, a right call wins coins from the wrong calls. The bots run in NEAR AI enclaves and every bot message is signed there, with the proof on chain.

Plain warnings

  • REAL is bought and sold on Rhea, a third party. Its price moves, and it can lose all of its value.
  • Realsies never holds your NEAR. Coins you put into the game can be lost in play.
